Passives

Torchforger's Covenant
After unleashing Elemental Skill Dawnbearing Songbird or Elemental Burst Shadowless Reflection, other nearby party members will gain the Lightkeeper's Oath effect for 20s: For Geo DMG dealt to opponents, CRIT Rate increases by 5% and CRIT DMG increases by 10%. Moonsign: Ascendant Gleam Party members affected by the Lightkeeper's Oath effect have their Elemental Mastery increased by 50.
Demonhunter's Dusk
Nightingale's Song's effects are enhanced: When there are 1/2/3 Hydro or Geo characters in the party, Nightingale's Song will further increase DMG. Increase in DMG is equal to 7%/14%/24% of Illuga's Elemental Mastery. If this aforementioned DMG is inflicted by a Lunar-Crystallize reaction, increase in DMG is equal to 48%/96%/160% of Illuga's Elemental Mastery.
Moonsign Benediction: Unwithering in Winter
When Illuga is in the party, the party's Moonsign will increase by 1 level.
